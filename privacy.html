Privacy notice

Prototype notice · Updated October 9, 2026

Resume content stays in your tab

The current editor processes your entries in browser memory. It does not upload resumes, store them in browser storage, create accounts, or call an AI service. Reloading or closing the tab clears the entries. Files you export remain wherever you choose to save them.

No analytics or advertising code

This version includes no analytics tools, advertising scripts, third-party fonts, or tracking cookies. Public hosting uses Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare may process IP addresses and basic request logs to serve and secure the website under its privacy policy. These infrastructure requests do not include the content entered into the editor.

Your choices

Use fictional data to explore the prototype. Use Clear entries to discard your draft. Copying an AI brief only places it on your clipboard; sending it to an external service is a separate action you control.

Future AI features

Claude API integration is planned but not enabled. This notice will be updated before features that send resume content to a server or AI provider are introduced.

Contact

A verified domain contact address has not yet been enabled. Do not treat an unverified address as a working support channel. It will be added before this website is considered ready for a startup application.
